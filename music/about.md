    <p>Rose Silver is a Pittsburgh-based soprano with choral experience singing sacred music and early music. She currently sings as a vocal fellow with <a href="https://www.thepittsburghoratory.org/saintgregoryinstitute">The Oratory Choir</a> as part of the St. Gregory Institute of Sacred Music at the Pittsburgh Oratory. She also sings professionally with the <a href="https://pittsburghcamerata.org/">Pittsburgh Camerata</a>.</p>

    <p>Her past ensemble work also includes singing with the <a href="https://www.bu.edu/chapel/music/marsh-chapel-choir/">Marsh Chapel Choir</a> (2018–2024), <a href="https://www.saintjamescathedral.org/music-and-art/cathedral-choir">St. James Cathedral Choir (Chicago)</a> (2022), <a href="https://www.nuchorus.org/">Northeastern University Choral Society &amp; Chamber Singers</a> (2017-2018, 2019-2020), <a href="https://ism.yale.edu/yale-repertory-chorus#:~:text=Performing%20twice%20a%20year%20at,staff%20may%20not%20be%20paid">Yale Repertory Chorus</a> (2016-2017), and various Yale Recital Choruses (2015-2017) for the degree recitals of second-year choral conductor masters students. On the concert stage she has appeared in Beethoven's Symphony No. 9 with the Boston Philharmonic Orchestra; Bach's St. John Passion with the American Symphony Orchestra; Handel's Dixit Dominus; Mozart's Mass in C Minor; and a broad span of Bach cantatas with the Marsh Chapel Choir. As a soloist she has sung Mozart's Requiem (2018) and Handel's Messiah (2017) with the Northeastern University Choral Society. Rose was introduced to the world of liturgical choral music at the age of eight years old as a chorister in the <a href="https://www.trinitynewhaven.org/music">Trinity Girls Choir</a> (2007-2015) at Trinity Church on the Green, New Haven under the direction of Walden Moore.</p>

    <p>She has also studied conducting under Katherine Chan and Nathan Reiff and served as Assistant Conductor of the Northeastern University Choral Society in 2019.</p>
